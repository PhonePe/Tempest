# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Tempest contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation
professional, respectful, and technically constructive. This applies to issues,
pull requests, reviews, discussions, community events, and private project
communication.

Acceptable behavior includes:

- discussing ideas and evidence rather than attacking people;
- giving actionable review feedback and accepting correction;
- respecting different experience levels, identities, backgrounds, and views;
- disclosing conflicts of interest that affect a technical or governance
  decision; and
- protecting private security reports and confidential user information.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption, bad-faith
misrepresentation, or publishing another person's private information without
permission.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to [oss@phonepe.com](mailto:oss@phonepe.com).
Do not use public issues for reports that identify a target or contain private
evidence. Maintainers will review reports promptly, limit disclosure to people
needed for a fair response, and avoid conflicts of interest where practical.

Responses may include a clarification, warning, content removal, temporary
participation restriction, or permanent exclusion. Deliberately false or
retaliatory reports may themselves lead to action. Decisions should be based on
documented behavior and applied consistently, regardless of a contributor's
role or seniority.

## Scope

This policy applies whenever a person represents the Tempest project or when
conduct has a direct and substantial effect on participation in the project.
Security vulnerabilities should be reported under [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md),
not through the conduct process.
